# Snow Rush

Snow Rush is a small 3D platformer where you play as a penguin and try to make your way through a snowy level!

This is my first Godot game, and I made it while learning game dev. It was really fun working on it and learning new things along the way. I wanted to add more stuff, but with exams coming up, I had to finish it a little earlier than planned. Still, I'm really happy with how it turned out!

## Gameplay

- Play as a penguin in a snowy level.
- Jump across platforms.
- Dodge snowballs thrown by snow golems.
- Collect snow flower along the way.
- Survive with 5 hearts and try to reach the end!
